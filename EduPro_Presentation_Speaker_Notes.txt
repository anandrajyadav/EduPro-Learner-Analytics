VIVA CORE ANSWERS:
1. Why descriptive analytics? Because the research objective is to understand existing behavior, not predict future behavior.
2. Why no machine learning? Prediction is outside the primary scope.
3. What is the master dataset? A unified dataset created by joining transactions with learner and course information.
4. Enrollment vs learner? Enrollment is a transaction; learner is a unique UserID.
5. Does enrollment mean completion? No, completion data is not available.
6. Does popularity mean quality? No, enrollment measures participation, not quality.
7. Does correlation prove causation? No.
8. Why SDG 4? The analysis provides evidence relevant to education access, skills demand and demographic participation.
9. Limitations? Dataset scope, no completion/outcome data, and descriptive analysis cannot establish causality.
10. Future scope? Completion, retention, prediction, recommendation, geography and longitudinal analysis.
