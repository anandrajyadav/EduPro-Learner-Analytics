EduPro Learner Demographics and Course Enrollment Behavior Analysis - SDG 4 Stakeholder Summary
===========================================================================
Total enrollment records analyzed: 10,000
Unique learners represented: 3,000
Unique courses represented: 60

SDG ALIGNMENT
The project primarily supports SDG 4 - Quality Education, with the strongest analytical connection to Targets 4.3, 4.4 and 4.5.

SDG 4.3 - ACCESS TO QUALITY EDUCATION
The analysis examines learner participation across age groups, gender groups and course levels. These descriptive patterns can help education stakeholders understand observed participation and course-access behavior.

SDG 4.4 - SKILLS FOR EMPLOYMENT
Course category, course type and course-level enrollment patterns provide evidence about areas of learning demand. Such evidence can support skill-development planning.

SDG 4.5 - EQUITY AND GENDER PARTICIPATION
Gender participation and gender-by-course analysis provide a descriptive basis for examining whether learning participation patterns differ across gender groups.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
EduPro enrollment data alone cannot measure the full SDG 4 framework. It does not directly measure learning outcomes, income, geography, disability, socioeconomic status, employment outcomes or educational completion.
Therefore, the project should be presented as a descriptive analytics study that can contribute evidence for education planning rather than as a direct SDG performance measurement system.

GOVERNMENT STAKEHOLDER VALUE
Government and education stakeholders could combine this type of enrollment analytics with official education statistics, socioeconomic information and learning-outcome data to develop stronger evidence-based education policies.
