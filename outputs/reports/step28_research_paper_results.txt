RESEARCH PAPER - RESULTS SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. DATASET PROFILE
The analysis examined 10,000 enrollment transactions involving 3,000 unique learners and 60 unique courses.

2. AGE GROUP ANALYSIS
The 26-35 age group recorded the highest enrollment volume with 4,799 enrollments.

3. GENDER ANALYSIS
The Female group recorded the highest observed enrollment share at 50.78%.

4. COURSE CATEGORY ANALYSIS
Data Science was the most preferred course category, accounting for 9.16% of enrollments.

5. COURSE LEVEL ANALYSIS
Beginner was the most preferred course level with 35.73% of enrollments.

6. COURSE POPULARITY
Data Analysis with Python recorded the highest number of enrollments.

7. TEMPORAL ANALYSIS
The highest monthly enrollment activity occurred in June 2025, with 899 enrollments.

8. SDG 4 CONNECTION
The study provides descriptive evidence relevant to SDG 4 themes including access to education, skills development and equitable participation.

9. INTERPRETATION
The results indicate that learner enrollment behavior varies across demographic groups, course categories, course levels and time periods. Cross-dimensional analysis provides a more detailed understanding of learner preferences than examining individual variables separately.
